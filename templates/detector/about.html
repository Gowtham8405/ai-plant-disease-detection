About PlantGuard AI

An academic computer vision initiative applying deep learning to automated agricultural crop pathology and foliar disease identification.

Project Motivation & Scope

Plant diseases pose a severe threat to global agriculture and food security. Early detection can help identify potential plant diseases, prevent widespread infection across fields, minimize crop yield losses, and provide relevant prevention information to farmers and agricultural researchers.

This platform serves as a college-level proof-of-concept demonstrating how modern Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) can be integrated with web technologies to deliver rapid, localized, and zero-cost crop health assistance without relying on paid third-party APIs.

95.33% Accuracy on the project's test dataset
38 Crop & Disease Classes Supported
70.0% Reliability Confidence Threshold
ℹ️ Academic Note: The 95.33% evaluation accuracy reflects performance measured on the project's curated unseen test dataset (3,145 samples). It is intended for educational demonstrations and should not be considered a guaranteed field diagnosis for all real-world environmental variations.

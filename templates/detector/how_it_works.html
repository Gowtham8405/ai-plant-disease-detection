How PlantGuard AI Works

An automated 3-step artificial intelligence workflow converting leaf photography into reliable agricultural diagnoses and actionable prevention insights.

Step 01
📸

Capture & Upload

Take a clear, well-lit, close-up photo of a crop leaf showing the affected surface or healthy foliage. Upload the image (JPG, JPEG, PNG, or WEBP, up to 10MB) via drag-and-drop or file selection.

  • Instant client-side image preview
  • Format & integrity validation
  • Secure server-side upload processing
Step 02
🧠

AI Model Inference

The image is resized to $224 \times 224$ pixels and processed by a fine-tuned MobileNetV2 Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) trained across 38 distinct crop categories.

  • Transfer learning with ImageNet weights
  • Extracts visual disease biomarkers & textures
  • Computes softmax probability distribution
Step 03
⚡

Instant Diagnosis & Guidance

The platform displays the identified disease, model confidence percentage, top-3 candidates, symptoms, causes, prevention advice, and basic recommendations.

  • 70% reliability confidence threshold enforcement
  • Comprehensive symptom & prevention profile
  • Automatic prediction history logging for accounts
